About me

Where I'm headed

I'm a Sociology major at Tulane University with minors in Political Science and Economics and a 3.88 GPA. Law draws me for the same reasons student government and campaign work do: rules and structures shape people's lives, and someone has to read them closely, argue about them honestly, and improve them.

On campus, my fellow delegates elected me Co-Chair of the Tulane Undergraduate Assembly, where I headed a working group that recommended reforms to TUA's structure. Before that, as a delegate, I started Project Pulse to show students what their representatives were working on. I'm also one of two undergraduate University Senators and serve on the School of Liberal Arts Dean's Advisory Board.

Off campus, I spent summer 2026 fundraising for Ethan for Kansas, a campaign that raised a record $2.2 million. Over three summers at Camp Sabra, I co-led 70 campers on two-week backpacking trips and received the camp's staff member of the year award.

In the classroom, my coursework spans sociology, political science, and economics. My academic projects range from a statistical study in R and a legal-historical analysis of Roman adultery law to a policy strategy for expanding contraceptive access in Kansas. You can also see the skills I’ve built along the way.
