01 Hormonal Contraceptive Access Policy in Kansas Activism and Lawmaking Methods: Policy researchLegislative historyAdvocacy strategyCoalition-building

I independently researched barriers to hormonal contraceptive access in Kansas, analyzing the state’s healthcare infrastructure, legislative history, and political landscape to identify obstacles to reform and opportunities for bipartisan support. Based on my findings, I developed a five-year, $1.25 million advocacy strategy to expand pharmacists’ prescribing authority through grassroots mobilization, strategic messaging, coalition-building, and legislative lobbying.

02 Nuance in Roman Adultery Law Roman Family Law Methods: Legal-historical analysisPrimary-source researchLegal argument

I conducted a legal-historical analysis of the lex Iulia de adulteriis coercendis, an Augustan law that transformed adultery from a private family matter into a state-prosecuted offense. Examining Roman legal texts, imperial rescripts, and juristic interpretations, I argued that the law sought to channel private vengeance into formal legal proceedings while reinforcing patriarchal authority, marital legitimacy, and legal distinctions between acts of passion and criminal homicide.

03 Protestants and the Protestant Work Ethic Social Statistics Methods: R / RStudioRegression analysisANOVASurvey data (GSS)

I conducted an independent quantitative study testing whether Protestant religious identity is associated with higher occupational prestige, drawing on Max Weber’s Protestant Work Ethic theory. Using R, I analyzed 2,148 responses from the 2016 General Social Survey through linear and multiple regression models, controlling for race and paternal occupational prestige, and compared model fit using ANOVA. I found no statistically significant association between Protestant identity and occupational prestige, while both control variables were significant predictors.

04 Somali Food Crisis: United Nations Intervention Proposal Engaging the United Nations Methods: Humanitarian policy analysisInternational organizationsPolicy proposal

I researched Somalia’s 2025–2026 drought and its humanitarian consequences, analyzing food insecurity, internal displacement, and the effectiveness of existing UN interventions. After identifying critical gaps in humanitarian funding, resource allocation, and displaced-person protection, I developed a three-pronged intervention strategy recommending expanded UN funding, anticipatory drought relief through the Food and Agriculture Organization, and a UNHCR-led protected movement system for displaced Somalis.

05 Influence of Classroom Technology Policy on College Student Engagement Introduction to Research Design Methods: Mixed-methods designSurvey designInterviewsStatistical testingTeam project

I collaborated with four students to develop a mixed-methods research proposal examining the relationship between classroom technology restrictions and undergraduate engagement at Tulane University. We designed a semester-long study combining quantitative Likert-scale surveys and qualitative interviews with students and professors, accounting for differences in class size, subject, and classroom dynamics. Our proposed analysis incorporated t-tests, chi-squared tests, and qualitative thematic analysis to evaluate engagement patterns across classroom technology policies.
