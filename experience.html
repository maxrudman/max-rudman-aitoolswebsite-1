Two experiences that shaped me

Ethan for Kansas · Summer 2026

Helping raise a record $2.2 million

The situation: Senator Ethan Corson's campaign needed to build support at every level, from national donors to neighbors on the doorstep.

What I did: As a fundraising intern I made thousands of cold calls, researched potential donors at the local, state, and national level, and helped prepare the candidate's letters to supporters and undecided voters. I also canvassed nearly 200 homes, almost all of them in our primary opponents' districts, and delivered more than 100 yard signs.

The result: Our team raised $2.2 million, setting Kansas fundraising records and outraising our primary opponents combined.

Camp Sabra · 2023–2025

Leading when the stakes are real

The situation: In the Masada unit, each session ends with a two-week backpacking trip across Colorado. As one of the few staff members on site, I was responsible for campers' safety miles from help.

What I did: I co-led 70 campers over two sessions, specializing in programming and community care. I handled camper emergencies and coordinated with park officials, bus drivers, off-site supervisors, and law enforcement. I worked my way up from counselor to Assistant Unit Head (overseeing five cabins) to Masada staff.

The result: Safe trips and campers who were supported throughout. I received the Terri Grossman Neshama Award as the camp's male staff member of the year, and I was selected as an FJC Cornerstone Fellow.
